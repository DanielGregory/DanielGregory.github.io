    AI-Driven Swing Trading Model
    Developed a machine learning model to predict 1-day price movements for any stock. Currently achieving consistent profits on many stocks, while stocks being predicted at less than a 50% rate have their predictions flipped, i.e. a stock being predicted at a 38% rate becomes a stock predicted at a 62% rate.
    Key Features:
    - Daily runs on S&P 500, predicting if a stocks price will increase or decrease.
    - Uses data driven techniques and online learning to adapt and improve upon itself over time.
    - Utilizes an SGDClassifier, which assigns weights to each varaible and updates them dynamically as new data is processed.
    Overall Performance: - For stocks with a 60%+ accuracy rating (typically 90-100 stocks), investing $5,000 daily yields an average profit of $200 over a 50-day period. Many of these stocks generate a daily return of approximately 0.8% to 1%.
